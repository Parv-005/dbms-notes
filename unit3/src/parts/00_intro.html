0How to use these notes

  • Syllabus covered: Database design: top-down approach (ER model), participation constraints, specialization, generalization and aggregation, relationship types, relationship sets, roles and structural constraints, weak entity types, transformation of ER schema to relational tables.
  • How Unit 3 is examined. In every end-sem Q3 is entirely Unit 3 (three 5-mark parts, attempt two). The same three kinds of question come every year:
    1. Design: a story (“a university / hospital / car-rental company …”) → draw the ER diagram, often “and convert it to tables with the minimum number of relations”.
    2. Convert: a given ER diagram → relational schema with primary and foreign keys, “minimum number of tables”.
    3. Theory with examples: weak entities, specialization / generalization / aggregation, overlap / disjoint, multivalued attributes, cardinality.
    Mid-sems ask 2-mark versions of the same.
  • Every ER diagram here is drawn from a definition, and every “convert to tables” answer is computed by a program that applies the mapping rules of section 8. It is then compared with the answer written by hand, and the generated CREATE TABLE statements are run in SQLite. If a schema says “5 tables”, the rules really give 5 tables.
  • Your class notes (rules 1–7 for ER → table, the A/B/C and M/P/N questions) are included where they fit: section 8 follows them rule by rule, and the class-notes questions are solved in section 9.
  • Boxes: Definition exact wording to reproduce, Example, Tip, Common mistake, Exam what is asked and how, Beyond syllabus useful extras (marked, safe to skip on a first read).
  • Sections 12–13 contain every Unit-3 PYQ with full exam-style answers, then harder practice questions. Section 10 is a one-page revision sheet and 11 has MCQs. Use the ▾ Answers button in the sidebar to open or close all answers.
