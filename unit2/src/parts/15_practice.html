15.4 Relational calculus

Practice 6TRC + DRC

Write in TRC and DRC: (a) names of pilots certified on every Airbus aircraft; (b) names of pilots who are certified only on aircraft with range > 2000.

Show answer

(a) TRC:

{ t | \exists e \in Employees (t.ename = e.ename \and \exists c \in Certified (c.eid = e.eid) \and \forall a \in Aircraft (a.aname\ LIKE\ 'Airbus\%' \implies \exists c2 \in Certified (c2.eid = e.eid \and c2.aid = a.aid))) }

DRC (Employees ⟨E, N, S⟩, Aircraft ⟨A, AN, R⟩, Certified ⟨E, A⟩):

{ \langle N \rangle | \exists E, S (\langle E, N, S \rangle \in Employees \and \exists A0 (\langle E, A0 \rangle \in Certified) \and \forall A, AN, R (\langle A, AN, R \rangle \in Aircraft \and AN\ LIKE\ 'Airbus\%' \implies \langle E, A \rangle \in Certified)) }

15.5 Counting, constraints and triggers

Practice 7How many tuples?

R(A, B) = {(1,10), (2,10), (3,20), (4,30), (5,NULL)}, S(B, C) = {(10,x), (10,y), (20,z), (40,w)}. Without computing everything, state the number of tuples of: R ⋈ S, R ⟕ S, R ⟗ S, R ⋉ S, πB(R) − πB(S).

Show answer
  • R ⋈ S = 5: B = 10 → 2 R-tuples × 2 S-tuples = 4; B = 20 → 1 × 1 = 1; B = 30 and NULL match nothing.
  • R ⟕ S = 7: the 5 matches + unmatched R-tuples (4,30) and (5,NULL).
  • R ⟗ S = 8: 7 + unmatched S-tuple (40,w).
  • R ⋉ S = 3: R-tuples having a partner: 1, 2, 3 (each counted once).
  • πB(R) − πB(S) = 2: {10, 20, 30, NULL} − {10, 20, 40} = {30, NULL}. (Set operations treat NULLs as identical values, unlike joins/comparisons.)
